# Game Description
In a rich fantasy medieval universe, rumors speak of a newly unearthed dungeon deep within the heart of an ancient elven village – Whispershade Enclave. Drawn by the promises of power and untold treasures, you’ve journeyed far to find it. But entry is not granted – it must be earned. You must face a brutal and deadly trial, designed to crush the unworthy. Succeed, and the mysteries of Whispershade await. Or fail, and your story ends in blood and ash. Will you rise and embrace the challenge, or turn back and let fear decide your fate?

# Story
You are an ordinary soul, living a quiet life in the cobbled town of Eldergrove. Your days are filled with the familiar: helping merchants in the market, listening to bards spin old tales by firelight, and gazing toward the horizon, wondering if there’s more out there meant for you.

Lately, you hear the whispers about a newly discovered dungeon, buried deep beneath the ancient elven village of Nyshalanor. They call it the Whispershade Enclave–where ghostly echoes of elven warriors patrol eternally, bound by a broken oath made in a forgotten war.  It’s said that unimaginable treasures lie within, enough to tempt even the most cautious heart. You feel something you haven’t in a long while–purpose.

You set out towards Nyshalanor, the famed village of silver-leaved trees and moonlit lakes. Upon arrival, your main priority is to gain information on how to enter the dungeon. Therefore, you are greeted with a choice: wander and explore the mystical streets, or converse with the villagers.

Whichever path you choose, you will find yourself at the official Guild Hall of Nyshalanor, where adventurers are made. You enter the Guild Hall and sign up to become an official adventurer.

The guildmaster warns you of the dungeon prerequisite. “Before you may set foot in Whispershade, you must prove your worth.”

Your trial lies just beyond the village in a moonlit clearing. There, in the hush of night, a baby Huskryn–a nightmarish beast that escaped the dungeon when it was first discovered. Though young, it is vicious and unrelenting–a taste of the danger that lies within Whispershade. This will be your first true battle. Only one of you will leave the clearing alive.

There are two outcomes to this battle. If you fall, the darkness consumes you, and your dreams of glory end in silence beneath the stars. If you succeed, bruised but alive, you have proven you have the strength to continue.

You return to the Guild Hall, where you are presented with your Adventurer’s License, etched with your name and class sigil.

You arrive at the entrance of the dungeon–a gaping maw of ancient stone, exuding a cold, unnatural air. A guard steps aside, nodding as you present your license. Now, you will make the final decision. As you stare at the path in front of you, you ask yourself: are you ready?

Ending 1: You decide to move forward. You grip your weapon and take your first step into the darkness. The echo of your footsteps is swallowed by the abyss. The adventure begins.

Ending 2: Your courage waivers. The darkness is too deep, the memory of the Huskryn too fresh. You turn away, bidding farewell to your dreams of becoming an adventurer. You return home–alive, but forever wondering what if.

# Battle Rules
- Each player in the party will attack the same enemy and they will choose an **action** when it's their turn.
- Actions require a **minimum dice roll** (on a **20-sided die**, or D20) to be successful.
- If the dice roll meets or exceeds the action’s required threshold, the **attack is successful**.
- **Successful damage** is calculated as:  
  `Damage = Dice Roll Value + Unique Attribute`
- Players and Enemy continue in turns until the party or enemy is victorious!

# Objective
Traverse the ancient elven village and discover a way to the location of the new dungeon, Whispershade Enclave by navigating decisions, class mechanics, and turn-based combat.
